My senior thesis explores how to reduce the gap between what people want from AI and how they express it in prompts. As AI becomes more common in daily life, many users still need help communicating their goals clearly.

We are developing a **Prompt Workshop** feature that detects underdefined prompts and works with users to improve them. The proposed process helps users identify missing goals, context, and constraints, then refine their request through guided questions.

The project is in progress. I will add prototype details and evaluation findings as the work develops.
